Project Judgment

Institutional Quant Terminal | Async Event-Driven | Actuarial Risk Management

turkeykai@MacBook-Pro: ~/quant_engine/v4_ultimate.py

Architecture & Decoupling

To fully utilize the M4 Pro chip's concurrency, the V4 version abandoned synchronous procedural code. The system is split into three independent microservices communicating via asyncio.Queue (Pub/Sub), completely eliminating UI lag caused by network I/O.

📡 1. Data Pump

The pure producer. Concurrently fetches 1-Min and 1-Day dual-timeframe market data for the entire watchlist, detects timezone closures, and pushes payloads to the queue.

🧠 2. Actuarial Engine

Consumes data to calculate benchmark risks, VWAP, Z-Scores, and ATR using vectorized operations, ultimately generating the signal dictionary.

🎨 3. UI Renderer

A 100% zero-dependency Native ANSI renderer built from scratch. Translates price series into character matrices via algorithmic interpolation, ensuring absolute stability.

Mathematical Core

Moving beyond simple moving averages, the system integrates rigorous statistical and actuarial models for a full-stack loop from market timing to capital management.

StatArb: Z-Score Momentum

Z = (X - μ) / σ

Calculates standard deviation multiples of current price deviations from the moving average. Combined with VWAP, it captures mean reversion points in extreme markets.

Tail Risk: 95% Parametric VaR

VaR = Capital × (1.645σ - μ)

Assuming a normal distribution of returns, it calculates the maximum potential daily drawdown at a 95% confidence level, quantifying tail risk into actual dollar values.

Capital Allocation: Kelly Criterion

f* = (bp - q) / b

Based on the strategy's historical win rate (p) and odds (b), the system calculates the optimal capital bet fraction (f*) to maximize long-term compound growth.
